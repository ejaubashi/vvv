# FORM: spaces that behave differently

Research date: 22 October 2026. This study starts from the actual application on `unravel-the-room`, rather than a new framework demo. The implemented next proposal is **Impossible thresholds**. Room memory, time changes, and sound remain proposals unless explicitly marked implemented below.

## Direction

FORM becomes more distinctive when a place changes its rules in response to a visitor: an opening contains somewhere else, a wall retains a fading trace, or a surface reveals another moment. Additional interface animation alone does not create that experience. Keep the visual language quiet enough to make those events legible.

The existing foundation is useful: one application clock, an optical compositor, three authored rooms, a licensed captured Pantheon, bounded viewpoint movement, reversible splat deformation, reduced motion, and still-frame export. There are also concrete constraints: capture lighting is baked; the Pantheon has incomplete coverage and a reconstructed floor; Spark uses the standard WebGL renderer while authored rooms use WebGPURenderer/TSL. A splat is not a collision mesh or a physically relightable material.

## Art and interaction studies

These are directions for original work, not claims of copying an artist or having reviewed a new exhibition. The official James Turrell, Olafur Eliasson, teamLab and Ryoji Ikeda sites could not be reached from this environment. Their URLs were attempted; they are **not evidence of a current exhibition or newly shipped feature**. Established references suggest useful questions: how much can an opening change the perception of light, how can atmosphere give a room presence, and how can a visitor's action remain legible within a larger system?

For current implementation evidence I read official project source and READMEs. [Codrops interlude](https://github.com/codrops/interlude) describes Astro/GSAP transitions with Three.js, [atelier-north](https://github.com/codrops/atelier-north) describes a minimal architectural landing page, and [OnScrollColumnsRows](https://github.com/codrops/OnScrollColumnsRows) describes GSAP-driven column/row choreography. OnScrollColumnsRows is an older study, even though its repository metadata changed recently. Repository descriptions and source are not a hands-on audit of their hosted demos. The transferable lesson for FORM is coordinated composition and clear transitions; a room should also respond spatially after the transition finishes.

Three art studies are especially suited to this application:

1. **The incompatible doorway.** Quiet warm stone frames a cold, structurally different place. As the visitor moves, near and far objects separate. The seam stays architectural and understated. A single contradiction is easier to read than many simultaneous distortions.
2. **Matter as a temporary instrument.** Unravel already establishes action and return. Add a small, localized, fading residue to an authored wall so the next encounter acknowledges a previous gesture. A reset and an obvious decay make the rule understandable.
3. **Time with provenance.** An aperture reveals another state of the same place. An authored weathering layer is labeled interpretation; a second photographic capture is labeled with its actual date and source. Never present a fabricated restoration as documentary history.

## Prioritized implementation proposals

| Proposal | Visitor experience | Rendering / content work | Main limit | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Impossible thresholds — implemented in this branch** | Open a doorway, look into the next authored room, deliberately step through | One extra scene, perspective-mapped camera, oblique destination near plane, depth-tested arch, bounded render target; destination graph adoption | Authored rooms only; one opening; no recursive worlds or light transport through the seam | Both renderer backends render correctly; foreground occlusion, view-ray alignment, entrance clipping, crossing, cancellation and disposal verified |
| **A room with memory — recommended next** | A touched wall keeps a fading trace when revisited | Store a small bounded list of world-space marks in session memory; surface shader blends roughness/emission; one shared decay clock | A splat has no reliable surface normal or collision mesh. Start on authored stone | A trace stays anchored as the camera moves, decays to zero, survives room changes, and clears on reset. No identity tracking |
| **Sound beyond the opening** | A distant tone gains presence through the doorway | Web Audio oscillator or licensed field recording, HRTF PannerNode, portal-aware gain/low-pass, explicit mute | No automatic acoustic simulation from photographic splats | Starts only after visitor action, stops on hide/exit, and respects volume/mute and audio context state |
| **Time inside matter** | Scrub a calm, localized change between two moments | First: authored wet/dry and patina masks. Later: aligned independent captures with provenance and an actual temporal correspondence strategy | A static capture does not contain unseen weather, restored surfaces, or alternate illumination | Clear authored/captured labels; alignment holds at more than one viewpoint; downloads and memory stay bounded |
| **Captured thresholds** | The Pantheon opens into an authored or separately captured place | Evaluate Spark portals vs native Three splats; author supporting doorway/depth geometry; align scale and camera; prepare additional licensed content | Baked lighting, translucent depth sorting, occlusion holes, multiple contexts and incomplete coverage | Render thin occluders and splats correctly on both sides; teardown is bounded; asset rights and size are explicit |
| **Breathing atmosphere** | Sun shafts and dust reveal volume subtly | Depth-aware fog or low-resolution volumetric pass; restrained dust instancing; real light direction in authored room | Extra passes, temporal artifacts and mobile fill rate; captured sunlight cannot simply be relit | Measured improvement at matched viewpoints with a low-cost fallback and reduced-motion alternative |
| **Material microstructure** | Stone, metal and cloth become convincing at rest | Better measured/PBR source textures, area-light approximation, authored weathering, carefully benchmarked AO / probe or voxel GI | Procedural grain alone does not equal photorealism; GI raises cost substantially | Side-by-side reference views, believable roughness/scale, stable highlights and measured GPU time |
| **Seated XR visit** | Look through the lens using natural head movement | WebXR session mode with stereo cameras and constrained origin; separate entry/exit UI | Browser/device coverage and photographed coverage | Comfortable seated path on actual hardware; no forced motion; ordinary desktop/mobile remains complete |

A true dynamic capture is a separate content pipeline. [4D Gaussian Splatting](https://github.com/hustvl/4DGaussians) is a CVPR 2024 research reference, not a newly released browser feature: its training and per-timestamp export workflow require temporal input. Adding a time slider to our one static SOG would not reconstruct missing moments. Use authored material states first, and obtain time-resolved capture before making documentary temporal claims.

## Recommended sequence

1. Ship the bounded doorway and verify it before multiplying worlds.
2. Add session-only memory to an authored surface, with a reset and finite decay. This gives visits continuity without collecting personal data.
3. Add optional spatial sound using a small original or properly licensed source. Sound should respond to the same doorway geometry and camera.
4. Prototype native Three splats in isolation with the existing capture. Compare SOG support/conversion, SH fidelity, deformation, sorting, context count, transfer size and actual GPU memory. Only then choose a shared captured/authored renderer.
5. Commission or acquire a second capture with matching viewpoint coverage and explicit redistribution rights. Build time and captured thresholds from real content rather than from unsupported promises.

## Measurement and evidence

The existing compositor copies room output into an origin-clean canvas. A second room therefore adds rendering cost before that copy. With this implementation the portal target is capped at a 1024-pixel long edge; only one destination is retained, and settled views stop rendering. Candidate resources are disposed when preparation is cancelled, and the destination graph transfers to the main room on crossing. No additional runtime dependency or capture download is introduced.

Software Chromium is useful for rendering correctness, deterministic input and lifecycle tests. It cannot establish a desktop/mobile GPU frame-rate claim. Before adding volumetrics or GI, collect actual frame GPU timestamps where supported, idle activity, first-opening latency, peak texture/geometry memory and a low-end mobile session. CPU submit time in the Details panel is not GPU render time.

Network access allowed GitHub API/raw source and npm. Several public product/artist sites were unreachable, so no unsupported live-demo rankings or browser-compatibility claims are made.
